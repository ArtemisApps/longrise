Account security

Authenticator App: 2FA | MFA

Offline authentication

Authenticator App - 2FA

Take your codes with you.

Manage time-based and counter-based verification codes in one place. Group accounts by purpose and protect access with a PIN.

Notification history

Recover Deleted Messages App

Return to a message after it disappears.

Keep a local copy of supported incoming notifications and media on your Android device. Revisit saved text, voice messages and attachments when the original is no longer visible.

Recovery depends on notification access and the files available after setup. Status Saver also lets you keep supported photos and videos.
