Longrise Global Co., Limited operates longrise.net. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the site, contact our team or use one of the three Android apps published under Authenticator 2FA Lab. The two authenticators offer different backup options.

Authenticator App: 2FA | MFA

Camera access allows the app to read enrollment information. Account labels and setup secrets are processed on the device to generate authentication codes. Camera permission can be withdrawn. If you export a backup to Google Drive or another destination, its operator handles the copy under its own privacy policy. Deleting local app data leaves exported copies at their destination.

Authenticator App - 2FA

The app uses camera access to read enrollment information and processes account entries, setup secrets and counter values on the device. Offline backups contain information that can recreate authentication codes. You control the storage location and any subsequent transfer. Camera access is revocable, and local entries and exported files can be deleted separately.

Recover Deleted Messages App

Notification access allows the app to retain local copies of supported incoming notifications, including sender names and message previews. Folder access allows it to save supported media and documents. Revoking access stops future reads covered by that permission. Local app history can be erased by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Exported files and copies sent to others remain under the control of their respective holders.

Sharing sends selected material to the receiving app. Direct Chat uses a phone number for a chat link. The Google Play declaration identifies phone number as information that may be shared for app functionality.

Ads, diagnostics and optional destinations

All three Android apps integrate Google AdMob, AppLovin, Pangle and Meta Audience Network for advertising, Google Analytics for Firebase for usage analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device and app information, and ad impressions or interactions to deliver and measure ads and detect invalid traffic. Analytics may process app events and usage information to help us understand how features are used. Crash diagnostics may include crash traces, app version and device information so we can investigate errors. The information processed can vary with device settings, consent choices and use of the app.

Provider privacy notices: Google, AppLovin, Pangle and Meta.

If an app offers advertising privacy choices, use its consent controls to review your selection. Android’s advertising settings may also let you reset or delete the advertising ID. These controls serve a different purpose from deleting saved codes or notification history. For a question about a specific release, send support the app title, version and country.

When you choose a backup provider, sharing destination or external link, its operator handles the material you send. Review its sharing settings and retention rules before transferring a backup or someone else’s message.

Website, purchases and correspondence

The website serves static pages through GitHub Pages. The host and network services receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested page to deliver it. Our pages contain no analytics scripts or contact forms.

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. For purchase support, provide the product name and relevant order reference. When you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to respond. Redact account secrets, message previews and payment details from screenshots.

Retention, security and international processing

Local entries remain available according to the app’s storage and your deletion choices. Exported files follow the retention rules of their destination. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve the request, handle disputes or meet legal obligations. No single retention period describes all of these copies.

Enrollment secrets and backup files can enable access to protected accounts. Keep them confidential.

Email, hosting and other providers may process information outside your country. Applicable protections depend on the provider, destination and law governing the processing.

Privacy rights and requests

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Where applicable, California residents may also request information about disclosures and opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. You may contact the relevant privacy regulator.

Email bravkidd@outlook.com with the app name, the action requested and enough context to locate any correspondence. We may ask for information needed to verify your request. For records stored only on your phone, deletion is controlled on that device. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian may use the same contact to request review.
