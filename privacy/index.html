Authenticator App: 2FA | MFA

Setup and camera access. Start two-step verification in the security settings of the account you want to protect. When it displays an enrollment QR code, open the scanner in the app and allow camera access. The code contains the setup secret used to generate future codes. Treat a screenshot of it like the secret itself.

Daily use. The app uses the setup secret and device time to produce six-digit codes, including offline. Account labels and groups help you identify entries. A master password can lock access to the app. To stop further scanning, open Android Settings → Apps → this app → Permissions → Camera and turn access off. The existing entries can still generate codes.

Exports and removal. If you start an export, choose the destination carefully. A copy saved to Google Drive or another cloud service is handled by that provider under your account settings. Review who can access it. To remove it, delete the file at the destination and check its trash. Clearing this app’s storage affects the device copy only. Confirm another sign-in method works before removing either copy.

The developer’s app-specific declaration reports no user data collected or shared under Google Play’s definitions. Google Play data safety details

Authenticator App - 2FA

Enrollment and code generation. Grant camera access when you choose to scan an enrollment QR code. This app supports time-based and counter-based codes. The enrolled secret, account entry and, for counter-based codes, the counter let the app generate codes offline. Groups organize entries and the optional PIN limits access on your device.

Moving devices. Use the app’s offline backup feature on the old device. Keep the backup private, use the restore feature on the new device and test a sign-in before erasing the original. A backup contains information sufficient to recreate codes. If you copy the file to another device or storage service, that creates another copy to manage.

Permissions and deletion. Revoke Camera in Android’s app permissions to stop scanning. To erase this installation’s entries, open its Android App info → Storage or Storage & cache → Clear storage. Remove exported files separately through the file manager or storage service you used. Android menu names vary by device.

The developer’s declaration reports no user data collected or shared under Google Play’s definitions. Google Play data safety details

Recover Deleted Messages App

Notification access. Enabling Android notification access allows the app to read incoming notification content, which may include a sender’s name and message preview. It saves supported items on the device so you can revisit a copy after the original disappears. Notification access is broader than permission to display notifications. Read Android’s access prompt before enabling it.

Folder access and media. When using media recovery or Status Saver, select the relevant folder in the Android picker. The app uses accessible files to save supported photos, videos, audio, documents, GIFs or stickers locally. An item can be missing if its notification was muted, its preview was hidden or its media was never available in an accessible folder.

Sharing and Direct Chat. Choosing a share destination sends the selected copy to the receiving app. Direct Chat opens a chat link for a number you enter. Google Play identifies phone number as information that may be shared for app functionality and declares no data collected. The declaration does not identify a specific recipient for every release. Google Play data safety details

Stop saving and remove copies. Search Android Settings for “Notification access”, select this app and turn access off. Review its media permissions in App info. Revoking access stops the corresponding future reads. To erase the private history already saved, clear app storage or uninstall. Check Files, Gallery and any destination you previously shared to for separate copies. Removing the original chat message does not erase those copies.

Ads, diagnostics and optional destinations

Earlier portfolio disclosures identify Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, AppLovin, Pangle and Meta Audience Network. Those disclosures describe advertising IDs, ad interactions, feature usage, device information and crash or performance reports used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and troubleshooting. They do not establish which components are active in a particular installed build. The product-specific Play disclosures are linked above.

If an app offers advertising privacy choices, use its consent controls to review your selection. Android’s advertising settings may also let you reset or delete the advertising ID. These controls serve a different purpose from deleting saved codes or notification history. For a question about a specific release, send support the app title, version and country.

When you choose a backup provider, sharing destination or external link, its operator handles the material you send. Review its sharing settings and retention rules before transferring a backup or someone else’s message.

Website, purchases and correspondence

The website serves static pages through GitHub Pages. The host and network services receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested page to deliver it. Our pages contain no analytics scripts or contact forms.

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. For purchase support, provide the product name and relevant order reference. When you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to respond. Redact account secrets, message previews and payment details from screenshots.

Retention, security and international processing

Local entries remain available according to the app’s storage and your deletion choices. Exported files follow the retention rules of their destination. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve the request, handle disputes or meet legal obligations. No single retention period describes all of these copies.

Use a device screen lock and restrict access to backups. If an authenticator setup secret may have been exposed, use the protected service’s security settings to replace that enrollment. A new code appearing on screen does not replace a compromised secret.

Email, hosting and other providers may process information outside your country. Applicable protections depend on the provider, destination and law governing the processing.

Privacy rights and requests

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Where applicable, California residents may also request information about disclosures and opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. You may contact the relevant privacy regulator.

Email bravkidd@outlook.com with the app name, the action requested and enough context to locate any correspondence. We may ask for information needed to verify your request. For records stored only on your phone, follow the removal steps above. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian may use the same contact to request review.
