Privacy Policy

This policy covers this website and the following Android apps published under Authenticator 2FA Lab.

  • Authenticator App: 2FA | MFA
  • Authenticator App - 2FA
  • Recover Deleted Messages App

Authenticator apps

The apps use account labels and setup secrets to generate verification codes on your device. Camera access lets you scan a setup QR code. You can manage camera permission in Android settings. Both apps’ Google Play Data safety listings declare no data collected or shared. If you export a backup, the copy goes to the location you select. A cloud provider you choose handles that copy under its own privacy policy.

Message recovery

With notification access enabled, Recover Deleted Messages App saves supported notification content on your device. Media access allows it to keep supported attachments. Its Play listing declares no data collected and lists possible sharing of personal information. Consult the Data safety section of the linked listing for the current disclosure. You can turn off notification access in Android settings to stop future capture.

Storage and deletion

Clear app storage in Android settings to remove local app records. Separately remove exported files from their destination, including cloud storage. Before clearing an authenticator, ensure you have another way to sign in to each protected account.

Website and support

This site uses no analytics scripts or contact forms. Hosting and network providers process connection details, including IP addresses, to serve pages. If you email support, we use your address and message to respond. To request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence, use the contact below. Rights depend on your location.

Purchases and changes

Google Play processes transactions under its own privacy policy. We will post revisions to this page with an updated date.

Advertising and diagnostics

The apps may include advertising, usage measurement and crash reporting components. Depending on the app, its version and your settings, these components may process advertising identifiers, device and app information, interactions with ads or features, and crash or performance events. These signals help deliver ads, limit repetition, detect invalid traffic and diagnose failures. An advertising identifier is distinct from an authenticator setup key or the contents of a saved notification.

Service providers

Providers identified in our portfolio disclosures include Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics), AppLovin, Pangle and Meta Audience Network. The mix can vary by app and release. Read the provider notices for their processing practices: Google, AppLovin, Pangle and Meta.

Your controls

Use Android settings to manage camera, file and notification access or to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Where an app presents advertising consent choices, you can use those controls to change your preferences. Disabling access can prevent the corresponding feature from working.

Retention and processing locations

Local records remain on the device until removed. Exported copies have their own lifetime at the chosen destination. Support messages are kept while needed to answer requests, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Providers may process technical information outside your country and apply their own retention schedules.

Regional privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent or opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. For requests concerning our processing, contact us below. We may need information to verify the request. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children and families

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact support with the app name and a description so we can investigate and address the request.

Contact

For questions or requests, email bravkidd@outlook.com and identify the app. Keep verification codes, setup keys and private messages out of your request.
